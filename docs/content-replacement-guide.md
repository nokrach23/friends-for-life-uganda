# Content Replacement Guide

All `placehold.co` images, leadership names, beneficiary details, impact numbers, partner logos, map, social destinations, donation instructions, and parts of the address are placeholders.

1. Collect written approval for final copy, verified statistics, contact details, and programme claims.
2. Obtain informed consent for every identifiable person, with stronger child-safeguarding controls for minors.
3. Export photography at appropriate crop ratios: hero 16:9, cards 4:3, profiles 1:1. Prefer AVIF/WebP with a JPEG fallback where needed.
4. Replace URLs and update alt text to describe the image’s purpose, not the filename.
5. Confirm beneficiary attribution preference; anonymity should be the default where disclosure could create risk.
6. Verify partner naming/logo usage and link only to approved destinations.
7. Re-check headings, line lengths, contrast, mobile crops, and page weight after replacement.

Before publishing statistics, document the reporting period, data owner, definition, and approval date. The prototype labels current figures as placeholders.
