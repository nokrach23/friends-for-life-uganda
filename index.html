Our Story

Friendship that grew into a mission of hope

Friends for Life Uganda was founded with a simple conviction. No child should face poverty, vulnerability, or hopelessness alone.

What began through friendship has grown into a Christ-centered mission serving vulnerable children, strengthening families, and helping communities build healthier and more hopeful futures.
