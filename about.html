About Us

Rooted in Christ. Living for each other.

We serve vulnerable children, strengthen families, and empower communities with practical care and the hope of the Gospel.

Who We Are

Love expressed through action

Friends for Life Uganda is a Christ-centered, non-profit organization committed to holistic and lasting transformation.

Every programme, partnership, and act of service reflects our desire to share God’s love in practical and meaningful ways.

Our Story

Friendship that became a mission

Friends for Life Uganda began with a simple conviction: no child should face poverty, vulnerability, or hopelessness alone. Friendship grew into a Christ-centered ministry committed to stronger families and hopeful futures.

Our Mission

Why we serve

To demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ by transforming vulnerable children, families, and communities through holistic care, quality education, Christian discipleship, and sustainable development.

Our Vision

What we see

Thriving children, empowered families, and transformed communities living with hope, dignity, and purpose in Christ.
